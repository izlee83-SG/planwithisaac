FAQs

Curious? Let's Clear Things Up.

Do I need experience with Claude or AI?+

No experience needed. The course is designed for beginners and walks you through everything step by step — from setting up Claude to running full content workflows.

What tools do I need?+

You'll need access to Claude (a free plan works to start), and optionally Figma and Notion. We cover setup for all tools inside the course.

Can I use my own brand and products?+

Absolutely. Every workflow in the course is designed to be applied to your own brand and product catalogue from day one.

Can I use this for a service business?+

Yes. While many examples use product brands, the systems for content repurposing, ad generation, and brand voice work equally well for service-based businesses.
